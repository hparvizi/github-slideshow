## (b) Evidence per recovery challenge
- Compartments: Rotliegend Indefatigable is reported to split into about 12 compartments with limited pressure communication (supports the penalty size; Permian, not Carboniferous). Supports C1 for Cavendish (DST on an apparent 1.8 bcf compartment, v3).
- Unconnected stacked sands / water-leg response: Tyne evidence above (early water in one well, none in another) supports C2/C3 as local-connectivity effects, relevant to Kilmar and Cavendish.
- Channel GWCs: no new source beyond v3's Kilmar reading.
- Damage, halite, low energy, hub truncation: no new source found this run (halite and scale: open).
- Complexity grouping as a method: OGA FQI (regression), Kuwait RCI, and Sodir proposal all support grouping or normalising by complexity; none is a gas-depletion test.
